🙋🏾 Honest answers

Questions people actually ask

Is Flex Budget live right now?
Not yet — what you can play with today is a fully interactive prototype with realistic demo data. Real bank connections and payments are simulated. Join the waitlist to hear the moment live features land.
How would account connections work?
Through consent-based, read-only connections with regulated financial-data providers. Flex would see balances and transactions to power your plan — it cannot move money without your explicit say-so. Availability of each institution depends on supported providers and regulatory approvals.
Can it really pay from multiple accounts at once?
That’s the flagship concept, and the demo shows the full flow. A live version depends on secure payment partners, bank APIs, Open Banking infrastructure, your authorisation and regulatory approval — so today we show it as a concept, not a claim.
Half my money is cash. Am I left out?
Not at all — that’s what the monthly record is for. Log what you made and any outside-app spending; it’s categorised, budgeted and included in your insights like everything else.
Does it force a 50/30/20 budget?
Never. Your plan is generated from your actual income, essentials, family responsibilities and goals. Irregular income? Flex suggests planning from your lowest recent month. Every number is editable.
Is the AI coach a financial adviser?
No — it gives educational guidance based on your own numbers. It’s not a licensed financial adviser and doesn’t replace professional advice.
Will I ever have to pay to use Flex?
No — budgeting, your lifestyle plan, manual tracking and basic insights are free forever. Premium (₦400/month or ₦4,000/year, first month free) is entirely optional and just adds automation like live sync and the AI coach. We ask you to upgrade only after Flex has already proven its value — never on a countdown, and never by making the free plan feel broken.
